Three Opus subagents, each in its own worktree on its own branch. Each one compiles, runs the JUnit tests, runs data generation, runs the GameTest server, and drives a muted client to its screen with a front-buffer capture. - The camera becomes a free camera with a position, a yaw and a pitch. No orbit pivot.
- Left-drag turns the camera in place. A gizmo drag does the same. A gizmo face click sets the view direction and puts the camera Y at the region center Y.
- WASD moves along the horizontal forward and right of the camera, like creative flight. Space moves up, Shift moves down. The speed is a constant 16 blocks per second with no sprint key.
- Middle-drag pans the camera along its right and up axes. One GUI unit of drag moves one block.
- One scroll tick moves the camera 16 blocks along its view direction.
- Movement keys are off while ImGui wants text input or a vanilla widget has focus.
- The Preview section gets a row with the camera position in world blocks and a Reset button. Reset puts the camera back where the region is centered at its current angles. Yaw and pitch stay.
- The preview opens framed as today.

**Batch 2, `claude/export-import`.** Files: a new `SettingsJson` client component with a JUnit test, the footer of `AerolitePresetScreen`, a pending-import field on `PreviewScreen`, two buttons after the seed row in the Preview section of `ImGuiPreviewPanel`.
- Export writes the settings through the existing codec as minified JSON to the system clipboard.
- Import reads the clipboard and parses it at once. A parse error shows as a red line of the codec message under the controls. A good parse is held as pending, and a line says it applies on Done.
- Done applies the pending settings in place of the edited ones. Cancel drops them. A new Import replaces the pending one.
- The Aerolite screen gets a second footer row with Export and Import. The panel gets the same two buttons.
- Because it only calls the codec, the feature follows the per-dimension layout once batch 3 merges.

**Batch 3, `claude/dimension-settings`.** Files: `AeroliteSettings`, `ShaftVariation`, a new `ShaftPlacement` record, a new `DimensionSettings` record, `ShaftLayout`, `ChunkPaint`, `PresetEdits`, `PresetSliders`, `AerolitePresetScreen`, a new `DimensionSettingsScreen`, the dimension sections of `ImGuiPreviewPanel`, `PreviewBuilder`, the tests and `TestSettings`.
- `ShaftPlacement` holds the two spacings and the column chance with its own codec. Default 48, 80, 0.3.
- `ShaftVariation` gains decay scale, decay threshold and erosion threshold with the existing validation. Defaults 64, 0.35, 0.5.
- `AeroliteSettings` becomes the cloud height plus a map from dimension to `DimensionSettings`. Cloud height stays top-level because it is not a shaft placement value.
- A 0.1.0 save folds its old top-level values into every dimension and every variation. The codec writes only the new layout.
