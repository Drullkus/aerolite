### Claude:

Pillars in the Nether now stop at Y 128, and each dimension has its own pillar range that you can set. The work is committed on `claude/pillar-y-range`. To apply it to `main` without a commit, run:

```bash
git cherry-pick -n main..claude/pillar-y-range
```

## What changed
- **Settings:** each pillar placement has two new fields, `min_y` and `max_y`. `max_y` is exclusive, and both values snap to the nearest multiple of 16.
- **Nether:** the shipped range is Y 0 to Y 128. Vanilla bedrock ends at Y 127, so 128 is the first multiple of 16 above the ceiling.
- **Other dimensions:** each one spans its full build height. The shipped files hold -2032 and 2032, the widest values a dimension allows, and the layout clamps them to the real build limits.
- **Old saves:** a placement without the new fields spans the full height, so existing pillars do not move.
- **Menus:** the Pillar Placement tab and the preview panel each have "Pillar Bottom" and "Pillar Top" sliders, in steps of 16. A slider that comes too near the other pushes it along.

## Choices I made
- **Smallest range is 64 blocks:** this keeps one slot for X pillars and one for Z pillars. Columns need at least 80 blocks, because their two pillars must have an empty slot between them.
- **Nether fingerprint updated:** `ShaftLayoutTest` failed on the Nether because its pillars move on purpose. I replaced only that value, and the Overworld and End values still pass unchanged.
- **Settings probe:** I added two states, the Nether Pillar Placement tab and a Nether preview from the side.

## Verification
- **Automated checks:** all 1489 JUnit tests and all 40 GameTests pass, and the build succeeds. A new GameTest confirms that the paint writes no block outside the range.
- **Client:** the muted client ran with the probe. The Nether tab reads Y 0 to Y 128, and the Overworld panel reads Y -64 to Y 320. The attached preview shows the Nether pillars in the lower half of the box.
- **Dedicated server:** it started without errors. I stopped it after startup.
- **Not checked:** I did not generate a real Nether world in the client. Please look at the ceiling in a new world.

## Open question: Nether bedrock
A column end can still replace bedrock at Y 0–4 and Y 123–127. No pillar block goes above Y 127, but a player can mine through a column to reach the Nether roof or the void. I added this to the open questions in `CLAUDE.md` and did not change the behavior.
