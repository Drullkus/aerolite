Power rule cells

0.80

Lower plot: the power rule value of each site along the line through P. The thick part is the least value. A cell border is where two curves cross.

  • Drag a site to move it. Click a site to ring it. Move the cursor over the map to place the point P.
  • The power rule value of a point for a site is the squared distance minus the cell weight times the squared cell size.
  • A point takes the site with the least power rule value.
  • The dashed circle of a site is its sphere. Its radius is the cell size times the square root of the cell weight.
  • A cross marks a lost cell: a cell that does not hold its own site.
  • This page is a 2D view. It has no rows above and below, and no Y scale.
